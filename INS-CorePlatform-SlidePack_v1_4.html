01 · Current situation

Four Recurring Issues Slow Core Change Down

These are process issues across Allianz, AzTech and Instanda, not a failing of any one team.

01

Mixed Priorities into Instanda

Fleet and DT raise and rank core change separately, so Instanda gets more than one order of work.

02

Classification Not Carried Through

Routes exist to separate bugs, build defects and CRs, but the outcome isn't always recorded and followed to closure.

03

Fragmented Records

Status sits across Jira, Freshdesk, Confluence, trackers and email, with no single linked view.

04

Requirements Not Ready at Submission

Requirements are often clarified or changed after the CR is issued, so estimates and plans are redone.

Instanda core platform · Change and Run · 02 Scope

Anyone can raise core change. OpsIT takes it to Instanda as one voice.

This proposal does not change who can raise a core platform change, or who has a say in it or its priority. It changes how those changes reach Instanda: through OpsIT (RetainedIT), which is accountable for the Instanda platform, its governance and compliance. Product build stays with Delivery.

Where they meet

Four joint gates

Joint · Instanda proposes, Allianz confirms

Bug, build or CR?

Instanda proposes a classification with a reason. AzTech and OpsIT confirm it, with a route to challenge. Only a confirmed CR enters the core path.

Joint · business, AzTech, Enterprise Architecture, OpsIT

Adopt or adapt?

Together decide whether to use native functionality or change the platform. OpsIT records the decision.

Joint · Architecture and AzTech sign off

Shared-asset design gate

Shared-asset changes need design and architecture sign-off before release. OpsIT checks the evidence is in place.

Joint · AzTech, Delivery and OpsIT

SaaS upgrade regression

Delivery tests live products. Go/no-go is agreed jointly, and AzTech can stop a release on technical grounds.

The test for the core platform path

Does the change alter how Instanda works for every LoB and every Instanda client?

Worked example · index-linking

Native index-linking is one figure. AZUK splits it across buildings and contents. That isn't a bug. The Adopt or Adapt review first checks whether a configuration or calculation workaround would do. If it won't, the route is Adapt and a CR is raised.
